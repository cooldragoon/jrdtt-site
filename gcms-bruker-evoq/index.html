Bruker EVOQ GC-TQ — High Sensitivity Triple Quadrupole

Attomole-level sensitivity. Compact footprint. MRM optimization in minutes.

Overview

The Bruker EVOQ GC-TQ delivers attomole-level sensitivity in a compact triple quadrupole platform designed for routine pesticide, PAH, and PCB analysis in food and environmental laboratories. Automated MRM optimization reduces method development from days to minutes with Bruker AutoMRM software. The vacuum-insulated, heated ion source eliminates cold spots for consistent analyte response. Compact dimensions reduce bench space requirements by 30% compared to competing GC-TQ platforms. Compatible with Bruker Compass CDS and third-party LIMS through open data formats. Optional dual EI/CI source for confirmatory analysis. This instrument is designed for analytical laboratories requiring reliable, reproducible results across routine and advanced applications. Key considerations for procurement include throughput requirements, detector compatibility, software ecosystem integration, and total cost of ownership over a typical 5-year instrument lifecycle.
